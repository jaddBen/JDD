🍎 Apple Health Integration

Bridge your iPhone fitness data to FitTrack — free, private, no backend needed

How it works: Apple HealthKit doesn't expose a web API, but you can get your data into FitTrack in two ways below — both are 100% free and your data stays on your device.
📁
Method 1: Import Export File
Manual · Easiest · 100% Free

Export your Health data from the iPhone Health app and upload the JSON/CSV here. FitTrack will parse it and import your workouts and steps.

  • Open Health app → tap your profile photo → Export All Health Data

  • Wait for the zip to generate, then share it somewhere accessible

  • Extract the zip and upload export.xml below

⚡
Method 2: iOS Shortcuts Automation
Automatic Daily Sync · Free · Setup once

Use the iOS Shortcuts app to automatically push your daily Apple Health data to your GitHub repo, which FitTrack reads on load.

  • Generate a GitHub Personal Access Token (PAT) with repo contents write permission at github.com/settings/tokens

  • Open Shortcuts app on iPhone → tap + → Add Action → search "Get Health Samples"

  • Add: Steps, Active Energy, Workout Minutes, Distance for Today

  • Add a Get Contents of URL action — set URL to your repo's GitHub API endpoint and paste your PAT in the headers (see template below)

  • Set as a daily automation — runs at midnight each night

  • FitTrack automatically reads data/health.json from your repo on load
